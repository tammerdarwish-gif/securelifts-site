## Launch Rules

- Start with Exact and Phrase match only.
- Do not launch Broad match until the campaigns have clean lead data and at least 20 qualified conversions in 30 days.
- Use Maximize Conversions only after conversion tracking is confirmed. Use Target CPA at $50 after enough qualified conversion volume exists.
- Keep Search Partners off at launch unless CPL is already stable under target.
- Keep Display Network off.
- Use location presence only: people in or regularly in the service area.
- Service area launch focus: Miami-Dade, Broward, Palm Beach. Expand only after CPL stays under target.
- Review search terms twice per week for the first month.
- Add negative keywords immediately from `public/google-ads-negative-keywords-securelifts.txt`.

## Keywords To Avoid For The $50 CPL Goal

Pause or do not launch keywords that imply shopping, DIY, employment, or low service intent:

- garage door parts
- garage door spring for sale
- garage door opener remote
- garage door opener manual
- how to fix garage door
- DIY garage door spring
- garage door jobs
- garage door technician salary
- used garage door
- Home Depot garage door
- Lowes garage door

## Why This Keyword Set Fits South Florida

The strongest lead intent in this market is urgent repair, broken spring, stuck door, and opener failure. Hurricane doors are important for organic and seasonal campaigns, but they are slower-consideration leads and should not be mixed into the $50 repair CPL campaigns. Keep hurricane/impact-rated doors in a separate quote campaign with different budget rules.
